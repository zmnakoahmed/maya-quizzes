Chapter 1: Space (questions 1 to 8)

Question 1

The book says the Sun is the star that keeps us warm. Explain in your own words how the Sun warms the Earth, and what would happen to us if the Sun stopped shining.

Question 2

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun, but Venus is the hottest planet. Explain why Venus is hotter than Mercury.

Question 3

Does Pluto have a moon? Tell me its name, and explain what a moon is.

Question 4

The book says Earth is the only planet with life, as far as we know. What does Earth have that the other planets do not? Name two things and explain why each one matters for life.

Question 5

What keeps all the planets moving around the Sun? Explain what would happen if it stopped.

Question 6

The book asks if there are robots in space. What jobs do robots do in space, and why are robots better than people for those jobs?

Question 7

Astronauts sleep in space very differently from how you sleep at home. Describe how they sleep, and explain why they have to do it that way.

Question 8

How does a rocket get into space? Describe what happens, and explain why it needs so much power.

Chapter 2: Planet Earth (questions 9 to 15)

Question 9

Question 10

What is a volcano? Explain in your own words what happens when a volcano erupts, and where the hot rock comes from.

Question 11

Question 12

Question 13

What is a glacier, and how is an iceberg different from a glacier? Explain both.

Question 14

The book shows how people damage the Earth. Choose two ways people damage the Earth, and explain what harm each one does.

Question 15

What is recycling? Explain how recycling helps the Earth, and name two things from your own home that can be recycled.
