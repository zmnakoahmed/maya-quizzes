Remember BODMAS

  1. Brackets first
  2. Orders (powers)
  3. Division and Multiplication, together
  4. Addition and Subtraction, together
The big rule: ÷ and × are equal. When they are in a row, work from left to right. Do not do the × first just because it comes later. Same for + and −.

Question 1

18 ÷ 3 × 2 = ?
Answer: 12. Go left to right. First 18 ÷ 3 = 6, then 6 × 2 = 12. If you do the × first you get 18 ÷ 6 = 3, and that is the trap.

Question 2

20 ÷ 4 × 5 = ?
Answer: 25. Left to right again. First 20 ÷ 4 = 5, then 5 × 5 = 25. Doing the × first gives 20 ÷ 20 = 1, which is wrong.

Question 3

5 + 6 × 2 = ?
Answer: 17. × comes before +. First 6 × 2 = 12, then 5 + 12 = 17. Brackets would change it, but there are none here.

Question 4

(5 + 6) × 2 = ?
Answer: 22. Brackets first. 5 + 6 = 11, then 11 × 2 = 22. See how the brackets change the answer from Question 3.

Question 5

30 − 18 ÷ 3 = ?
Answer: 24. ÷ comes before −. First 18 ÷ 3 = 6, then 30 − 6 = 24.

Question 6

8 ÷ 0.4 × 0.5 = ?
Answer: 10. This is the same shape as your Kumon question. Left to right: first 8 ÷ 0.4 = 20, then 20 × 0.5 = 10. If you multiply 0.4 × 0.5 first you get 8 ÷ 0.2 = 40, and that is the mistake to watch for.

Question 7

3 + 2 × 5 − 10 ÷ 5 = ?
Answer: 11. Do × and ÷ first: 2 × 5 = 10 and 10 ÷ 5 = 2. Then it is 3 + 10 − 2. Work left to right: 3 + 10 = 13, 13 − 2 = 11.

Question 8

(7 − 4) ÷ 0.3 = ?
Answer: 10. Brackets first: 7 − 4 = 3. Then 3 ÷ 0.3 = 10, because 0.3 goes into 3 ten times.
