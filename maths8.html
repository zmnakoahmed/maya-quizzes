Watch out 1: adding the carry.
Last time, 28 × 9 went wrong in the last step. Here it is, slowly:

9 × 8 = 72. Write the 2, carry the 7.
9 × 2 = 18. Now ADD the carry: 18 + 7 = 25.
So the answer is 252. Say the carry out loud when you add it: "eighteen plus seven is twenty-five."
If you forget the carry, or add it to the wrong digit, the answer comes out wrong even though the times tables were right.
Watch out 2: the zero goes on the RIGHT.
Last time, 47 × 26 went wrong in the middle step. Here it is, slowly:

47 × 6 = 282.
47 × 20 = 940. It is 47 × 2 = 94, and then a zero on the right, because it is twenties, not twos.
940, NOT 094! The zero on the left means 94, which is ten times too small.
Add them: 282 + 940 = 1222.
Every time you multiply by tens, put a zero at the end of that line first.
First, the multiplying itself.
Get the digits right first, then put the point in.

How to multiply two big numbers: 25 × 46
Step 1: 25 × 6 = 150.
Step 2: 25 × 40 = 1000. (46 is 40 and 6.)
Step 3: add them: 150 + 1000 = 1150.
Always do it in small pieces, and add the pieces at the end.
What is 0.7?
Imagine a chocolate bar with 10 pieces. 0.7 means 7 of those 10 pieces.
7 green pieces out of 10. That is 7 tenths, we write it 0.7.

And 0.07 means 7 hundredths. Imagine a big square with 100 tiny pieces. Only 7 are green.
7 green pieces out of 100. That is much smaller than 0.7!
Step 1. Hide the decimal points. Multiply the numbers like whole numbers.
Example: 54.6 × 0.7 becomes 546 × 7 = 3822.
Step 2. Count the digits after the decimal point in BOTH numbers, and add them up.
54.6 has 1 digit after the point, 0.7 has 1. Together that is 2.
Step 3. Put the decimal point in your answer so it has that many digits after it.
3822 with 2 digits after the point is 38.22. Done!

When you run out of digits: 1.65 × 0.03
Digits: 165 × 3 = 495. Decimal places: 2 + 2 = 4.
But 495 only has 3 digits! Add a zero at the front:
0.0495
The zero in front is the trick. It makes the number small, as it should be.
The bigger ones: 0.34 × 3.6
Split 3.6 into 3 and 0.6.
0.34 × 3 = 1.02
0.34 × 0.6 = 0.204, NOT 2.04! Remember, 0.6 means 6 tenths, so the answer is 10 times smaller than 0.34 × 6.
Add them: 1.02 + 0.204 = 1.224.
