Learn how to do it first

Take your time with this part. Read it slowly. There is no timer here.

Box 1: Times by 10, 100, 1000: move the point RIGHT.
One place for each zero.

7.8 × 10 = 78. 7.8 × 100 = 780. 7.8 × 1000 = 7800.
The point goes right, the number gets bigger.
Box 2: Divide by 10, 100, 1000: move the point LEFT.
One place for each zero.

7.8 ÷ 10 = 0.78. 7.8 ÷ 100 = 0.078. 7.8 ÷ 1000 = 0.0078.
Remember: move the point LEFT for divide, move it RIGHT for times.
Box 3: Zeros go at the END, never after the point.
The point HOPS, it does not stay still while you add zeros.

3.4 × 1000: the point hops right 3 times, 3.4 goes to 34, then 340, then 3400. Not 34.000.
6.3 × 100: the point hops right 2 times, 6.3 goes to 63, then 630. Not 63.00.
Box 4: Divide by 30, 40 or 400: two steps, write both down.
4.8 ÷ 40: first 48 ÷ 4 = 12, then divide by 10, the point hops left once: 0.12.

Do not stop after the first step. The second step is the whole point of the question.
