Maya's Decimal Division Lesson

Division with decimals. Four rules, and they all come from one big idea. Read it slowly, try the sums in your head, then try the practice at the end. Harry is your assistant. Tap the speaker to hear each rule.

Harry: Hi Maya! Harry here. Division makes things smaller, so the point moves LEFT. That is the whole secret.

1. The one big idea

Dividing means sharing, and sharing makes things smaller. Multiplying means making more, and that makes things bigger.

Think of money.
€3.60 shared by 3 friends is €1.20 each. Each friend gets less than €3.60.

So when you divide, your answer must be SMALLER than the number you started with. If your answer is bigger, the point went the wrong way.

2. Rule 1: Divide by 10, 100, 1000

Move the point LEFT. 1 place for 10, 2 places for 100, 3 places for 1000.

Count the zeros, move the point that many places left.
10 has 1 zero, so move left once. 100 has 2 zeros, so move left twice. 1000 has 3 zeros, so move left three times.
2.65 ÷ 10 = 0.265
The point jumps left once, and a 0 goes in front to hold its place.

2.65 ÷ 100 = 0.0265
Left twice.

2.65 ÷ 1000 = 0.00265
Left three times.
26.5 ÷ 10 = 2.65
26.5 ÷ 100 = 0.265
26.5 ÷ 1000 = 0.0265

265 ÷ 10 = 26.5
265 ÷ 100 = 2.65
265 ÷ 1000 = 0.265
265 ÷ 10000 = 0.0265
Watch out: zeros on the left do NOT move the point.
For 2.65 ÷ 10 you wrote 265. The point just vanished, and 265 is much bigger than 2.65. That cannot be right, division makes things smaller.

For 265 ÷ 100 you wrote 00.265. The zeros in front did nothing. The answer is 2.65, the point moved left twice, from after the 5 to before the 6.

Only the point itself moves, and it moves left.

3. Rule 2: Multiply by 10, 100

This is the opposite way. Multiplying makes things bigger, so move the point RIGHT.

1.2 × 10 = 12
Not 1.20. The point moves right once and disappears, because 12 has no point left to show.

1.2 × 30 = 36
Do it in two easy steps. First 1.2 × 3 = 3.6, then × 10 = 36.
0.42 × 10 = 4.2
0.42 × 100 = 42
0.42 × 200 = 84
First 0.42 × 2 = 0.84, then × 100 = 84.
Well done! Your 0.42 × 2 = 0.84, 0.42 × 10 = 4.20, 0.42 × 100 = 42.00 and 0.42 × 200 = 84.00 were all right.

4. Rule 3: Decimal divided by a whole number

Hide the point, divide, put the point back in the same place.

Step 1. Hide the point. Step 2. Divide like whole numbers. Step 3. Put the point back where it was.
3.6 ÷ 3 = 1.2
Hide the point: 36 ÷ 3 = 12. Put the point back in the same place: 1.2.

Money check: €3.60 shared by 3 is €1.20 each, not €3.20. Your answer was 3.2, which is bigger than the sharing should give. The point stayed in the wrong place.
3.6 ÷ 30 = 0.12
First 3.6 ÷ 3 = 1.2, then ÷ 10 = 0.12. The point moves left once.

3.6 ÷ 300 = 0.012
First 3.6 ÷ 3 = 1.2, then ÷ 100 = 0.012. The point moves left twice.

5. Rule 4: Decimal divided by a decimal

Make the divisor whole first. The divisor is the number after the ÷ sign, the one you divide by.

Step 1. Move the divisor's point right until it has no point left.
Step 2. Move the other number's point right the SAME number of places.
Step 3. Now divide as whole numbers.
8.61 ÷ 2.1 = 4.1
Move both points right once: 86.1 ÷ 21 = 4.1. You got this one right.

3.45 ÷ 1.5 = 2.3
Move both points right once: 34.5 ÷ 15 = 2.3. Right.

46.5 ÷ 1.5 = 31
Move both points right once: 465 ÷ 15 = 31. Right.
6.72 ÷ 0.21 = 32
Move both points right twice: 672 ÷ 21 = 32. Not 3.2, that is ten times too small.

7.56 ÷ 0.63 = 12
Move both points right twice: 756 ÷ 63 = 12. Not 0.12, that is a hundred times too small.

1.96 ÷ 0.14 = 14
Move both points right twice: 196 ÷ 14 = 14. Not 0.14.
See the pattern? In this section your answers were too small because the point stayed too far left.

Check with sense: 7.56 shared by about a half is about 12. It cannot be 0.12. If your answer looks far too small, the point needs to move right.

6. The big trap: do NOT count decimal places in division

In multiplication you count the decimal places in both numbers and move the point left that many. In division you do NOT do that. Making the divisor whole already fixes the point. The answer you get after dividing IS the answer. Do not move its point again.

From your own Quiz 10, look at what happened:

2.4 ÷ 0.6 = 4
You worked out 24 ÷ 6 = 4 in your working, then typed 0.4. The 4 IS the answer. Do not move it.

5.4 ÷ 0.9 = 6
54 ÷ 9 = 6. The 6 IS the answer, not 0.6.

8.61 ÷ 2.1 = 4.1
You worked out 86.1 ÷ 21 = 4.1 in your working, then typed 0.041. The 4.1 IS the answer.

6.72 ÷ 0.21 = 32
672 ÷ 21 = 32. The 32 IS the answer, not 0.0032.

7.56 ÷ 0.63 = 12
756 ÷ 63 = 12. The 12 IS the answer, not 0.0012.

1.96 ÷ 0.14 = 14
196 ÷ 14 = 14. The 14 IS the answer, not 0.0014.

And dividing by 10: 2.65 ÷ 10 = 0.265. Move the point left ONCE. Do not take the point away, that gives 265, which is a thousand times too big.
