Remember BODMAS
1. Brackets first
2. Orders (powers)
3. Division and Multiplication, together
4. Addition and Subtraction, together
The big rule: ÷ and × are equal. When they are in a row, work from left to right. Do not do the × first just because it comes later. Same for + and −.

Question 1

12 + 8 ÷ 2 × 3 − 5 = ?

Answer: 19. Do ÷ and × first, left to right: 8 ÷ 2 = 4, then 4 × 3 = 12. Now it is 12 + 12 − 5 = 19. The trap is doing (12 + 8) ÷ 2 first, which gives 25.

Question 2

20 − (6 + 2) × 2 = ?

Answer: 4. Brackets first: 6 + 2 = 8. Then 8 × 2 = 16. Then 20 − 16 = 4. The trap is doing (20 − 8) × 2 = 24.

Question 3

7.5 ÷ 0.5 × 2 − 4 = ?

Answer: 26. Left to right: 7.5 ÷ 0.5 = 15, then 15 × 2 = 30, then 30 − 4 = 26. If you forget the × 2 you get 15 − 4 = 11.

Question 4

(4 1/2 − 2 1/4) ÷ 0.5 × 2 = ?

Answer: 9. Brackets first: 4 1/2 − 2 1/4 = 2 1/4. Then left to right: 2.25 ÷ 0.5 = 4.5, then 4.5 × 2 = 9. This is the same shape as your Kumon question, with fractions and decimals together.

Question 5 - Spot the mistake!

Sara worked out 18 − 6 ÷ 2 × 3 and got 18. What is the correct answer?

Answer: 9. Sara did 18 − 6 first, which is the mistake. ÷ and × come before −. First 6 ÷ 2 = 3, then 3 × 3 = 9, then 18 − 9 = 9. Her way, (18 − 6) ÷ 2 × 3, gives 18.

Question 6 - Word problem

Maya buys 4 packs of pens at €2.50 each and a notebook for €3. She pays with €20. How much change does she get?

Answer: €7. First the pens: 4 × €2.50 = €10. Then add the notebook: €10 + €3 = €13. Then the change: €20 − €13 = €7. €13 is the total cost, not the change.

Question 7

100 ÷ (5 × 2) + 3 × 4 − 6 = ?

Answer: 16. Brackets first: 5 × 2 = 10, so 100 ÷ 10 = 10. Then 3 × 4 = 12. Now it is 10 + 12 − 6 = 16. If you forget the − 6 at the end you get 22.

Question 8

(2/3 + 1/6) × 12 − 8 = ?

Answer: 2. Brackets first: 2/3 + 1/6 = 4/6 + 1/6 = 5/6. Then 5/6 of 12 = 10. Then 10 − 8 = 2. If you forget the − 8 you get 10.
