Learn how to do it first

Take your time with this part. Read it slowly. There is no timer here.

Box 1: Times: forget the points, multiply, then count the places.
Forget the points and multiply the whole numbers. Then add up the digits after the point in both numbers, and put the point that many places from the right.

5.4 × 1000: 54 × 1000 = 54000. Count: 1 place. Point 1 place from the right: 5400. Not 540.
Box 2: Divide by 10 and 1000: the digits stay, add the zeros to the count.
Forget the points, the digits stay the same. Count the places in the number you started with, then add the zeros in 10 or 1000.

36 ÷ 1000: digits stay 36. Count: 0 plus 3 zeros, 3 places. Point 3 places from the right: 0.036. Not 0.36.
Box 3: Divide by 1000: do NOT do long division.
In the last quiz, three of the four divide by 1000 questions were wrong by one place. Long division makes the point land in the wrong place. Count the places instead.

48 ÷ 1000: 48 has 0 places. Add the 3 zeros of 1000. 0 + 3 = 3 places. Point 3 places from the right: 0.048. Not 0.48. If your answer has only 2 places after the point, it is wrong, count again.
Box 4: Count out loud, one place at a time.
Say the count as you write the point.

2.8 ÷ 10: digits stay 28. Count: 1 plus 1, 2 places: 0.28. Not 0.028.
Box 5: Divide by 30, 40 or 400: two steps, write both down.
8.1 ÷ 30: first divide by 3, 81 ÷ 3 = 27, 1 place, so 2.7. Then divide by 10, 2 places, so 0.27. Not 0.027.

Do not stop after the first step. The second step is the whole point of the question.
Box 6: Decimal divided by decimal: subtract the counts. Long division: point straight up.
2.4 ÷ 0.6: forget the points, 24 ÷ 6 = 4. Top places 1 minus bottom places 1 = 0, so the point does not move: 4.

0.5 ÷ 4: long division, put the point straight up first, then divide: 0.125. Do not forget the zero after the point.
