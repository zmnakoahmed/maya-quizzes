Question 1

What joins your muscles to your bones?

Tendons join your muscles to your bones. Ligaments hold your bones together.

Question 2

How many bones do you have in your hand?

You have 27 bones in your hand. They have long names, like metacarpal.

Question 3

What are the three types of blood cells?

There are three types of blood cells: red, white and platelets.

Question 4

What do white blood cells do?

White blood cells hunt down germs that cause infections and gobble them up.

Question 5

What do platelets do around a cut?

Platelets clamp around a cut. They trap red blood cells and form a scab.

Question 6

What happens in a heart attack?

Heart attacks happen when a blood vessel on the heart gets blocked.

Question 7

How does oxygen get from red blood cells into other cells?

Oxygen is absorbed from red blood cells through capillary walls and into other cells.

Question 8

What do artery walls do?

Artery walls squeeze and relax to push blood through.

Question 9

What is a reflex action?

Sometimes you do stuff without using your brain. That is called a reflex action.

Question 10

How do doctors test your reflexes?

Doctors test our reflexes by tapping our knee. Your leg should jerk up!
