Learn how to do it first

Multiplication: adding the carry in the multiply step.
Work 57 × 8 slowly:

7 × 8 = 56. Write the 6, carry the 5.
5 × 8 = 40. Now ADD the carry: 40 + 5 = 45.
So the answer is 456. Say it out loud: "forty plus five is forty-five."
If you forget the carry, the times tables were right but the answer is still wrong.
Multiplication: adding the carry when you add the two lines.
Work 46 × 28 slowly:

46 × 8 = 368.
46 × 20 = 920, zero on the right.
Add them: 368 + 920 = 1288.
When you add, carry the tens too: 8 + 2 = 10, write 0, carry 1.
What is 0.7?
Imagine a chocolate bar with 10 pieces. 0.7 means 7 of those 10 pieces.
7 green pieces out of 10. That is 7 tenths, we write it 0.7.

And 0.07 means 7 hundredths. Imagine a big square with 100 tiny pieces. Only 7 are green.
7 green pieces out of 100. That is much smaller than 0.7!
Step 1. Hide the decimal points. Multiply the numbers like whole numbers.
Example: 54.6 × 0.7 becomes 546 × 7 = 3822.
Step 2. Count the digits after the decimal point in BOTH numbers, and add them up.
54.6 has 1 digit after the point, 0.7 has 1. Together that is 2.
Step 3. Put the decimal point in your answer so it has that many digits after it.
3822 with 2 digits after the point is 38.22. Done!

Division Rule 1: divide by 10 or 100, move the point LEFT.
Dividing makes things smaller, so the point moves left.

2.65 ÷ 10 = 0.265. The point jumps left once.
2.65 ÷ 100 = 0.0265. The point jumps left twice.
Count the zeros, move the point that many places left.
Division Rule 2: decimal divided by a whole number.
Hide the point, divide, put the point back in the same place.

3.6 ÷ 3: hide the point, 36 ÷ 3 = 12, put the point back, 1.2.
Money check: 3.60 shared by 3 is 1.20 each.
Division Rule 3: decimal divided by a decimal.
Make the divisor (the number AFTER the ÷ sign) whole first. Move its point right, and move the other number's point right the SAME number of places. Then divide.

6.72 ÷ 0.21: move both points right twice, 672 ÷ 21 = 32.
7.56 ÷ 0.63: move both points right twice, 756 ÷ 63 = 12.
